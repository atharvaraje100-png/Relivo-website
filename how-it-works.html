How it works

Go live on your current number. No migration needed.

Keep your current phone number. Keep your booking tools. Just forward your line and Relivo handles the rest.

01

Forward your line

Keep the number your patients already know. Relivo sits behind your line and catches missed, busy, and after-hours calls — no hardware required.

02

Load your treatment menu

Add your services, pricing ranges, and provider schedules. Review and adjust before going live.

03

Relivo answers live calls

It checks real-time availability against your calendar and books consultations directly — reschedules and cancellations too, based on your rules.

04

Clinical questions get escalated

Anything outside scheduling — dosing, side effects, complications — is flagged for a licensed team member. Relivo is built to route medical questions to your team instead of guessing.

05

Your team gets the full picture

Transcript, intake notes, and next action land in your dashboard and inbox automatically. Nobody starts from scratch.

Where Relivo draws the line

What Relivo is built not to do.

Aesthetic medicine calls involve real clinical judgment. Relivo is built to know the difference between a scheduling question and one that needs a licensed provider.

Give medical advice or dosing guidance

Diagnose symptoms or complications

Promise pricing outside your approved ranges

Refuse a human handoff when a caller asks for one
