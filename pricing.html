Pricing

Pricing built around your call volume and workflow.

Every Relivo setup is configured around your business, including call handling, booking workflows, transfers, escalation rules, and operating hours. After a short discovery call, you receive a clear custom proposal.

Get Custom Pricing
Why it's customized

No two front desks handle calls the same way.

A single-location spa that needs after-hours coverage and a multi-location group with call routing and clinical escalation are running two different phone operations. A flat plan would either overcharge one or undersell the other — so your price is set by what your setup actually involves.

Call volume

How many calls your line takes in a typical week, including seasonal surges.

Hours of coverage

After-hours only, overflow during treatment hours, or full 24/7 answering.

Booking workflows

How consultations get scheduled, confirmed, and rescheduled in your practice.

Call routing

Where calls and callers go — front desk, on-call provider, or a specific location.

Integrations

How Relivo connects to the scheduling tools your team already uses.

Escalation rules

Which questions are answered on the call and which go straight to your team.

Number of locations

Per-location menus, calendars, and routing for groups with more than one site.

Custom scripting

How closely Relivo's language follows your treatment menu and house style.

What happens next

From first conversation to a written proposal.

Clicking “Get Custom Pricing” takes you to a short form and a scheduling step — here's what follows.

01

Discovery call

A short call about your call volume, coverage hours, booking tools, and how calls should be routed and escalated.

02

Configuration design

We map Relivo to your operation — call handling, booking workflow, transfers, escalation rules, and operating hours.

03

Written proposal

You receive a clear proposal with the price for your configuration and exactly what's included, to review on your own time.
