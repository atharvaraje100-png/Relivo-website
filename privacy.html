Privacy Policy

Last updated September 30, 2026

This policy explains what information Relivo collects through this website — including the demo request form and the live voice demos — how it is used, and the choices you have. It covers this website only. If your medspa uses Relivo to answer patient calls, how that call data is handled is set out in your service agreement.

Information we collect

Demo request form

When you request a demo we collect your name, medspa name, email address, phone number, number of locations, and anything you write about what you're hoping to solve. We use this to prepare for and follow up on your demo.

Scheduling

Demo times are booked through Cal.com, embedded on our contact page. Cal.com receives the details you enter in its booking form and handles its own confirmation emails under its own privacy policy.

“Get a call on your phone” demo

If you ask Relivo to call you, we collect your name and phone number and a record of your consent to receive that call. Your phone number is used to place one automated demonstration call using an AI-generated voice. We do not use it for marketing calls or texts, and we do not sell it.

Browser voice demo

If you start a voice demo in your browser, we collect the business details you type (business name, city, hours, and services, plus your name if you give it) so the AI can use them on the call. With your permission, your browser sends your microphone audio to our voice provider for the duration of the call, and a live transcript is shown on screen.

Technical information

Like most websites, our hosting provider receives standard request information such as your IP address and browser type. We use IP addresses and phone numbers for rate limiting, so the live demos can't be abused to place unwanted calls.

How we use information

  • To respond to demo requests and prepare a proposal for your practice.
  • To run the live demos you start, and only those demos.
  • To prevent abuse, such as repeated or automated demo requests.
  • To keep the website secure and working properly.

We don't sell personal information, and we don't use it for third-party advertising.

Service providers

We rely on a small number of providers to run this website. Each receives only what it needs for its part of the job:

  • Vercel — website hosting and server functions.
  • Retell AI — the voice technology behind the phone and browser demos. It processes call audio, transcripts, and the details you provide for the call.
  • Resend — delivers demo-request notifications to our team's inbox.
  • Cal.com — demo scheduling.
  • Upstash — short-lived rate-limiting counters keyed by IP address or phone number.
  • Google Fonts — web fonts, which are loaded from Google's servers.

Calls and recordings

Demo calls are handled by an AI voice agent, which identifies itself as AI. Our voice provider may retain audio and transcripts of demo calls to operate and troubleshoot the service. Please don't share medical or other sensitive personal information during a demo — the demos are for evaluating the product, not for booking real treatment.

Retention

We keep demo-request details for as long as they're useful for our conversation with you, and delete them on request. Rate-limiting counters expire automatically, usually within hours.

Your choices

You can ask us to access, correct, or delete the personal information we hold about you by emailing relivoai@gmail.com. Depending on where you live, you may have additional rights under local privacy law; we'll honor those requests as required.

Children

This website is intended for businesses and isn't directed to children under 13. We don't knowingly collect their information.

Changes

If we change this policy, we'll update the date at the top of this page.
